Privacy policy

YSS Ventures LLP · Effective 19 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by YSS Ventures LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in Mumbai, India. We are an automation agency: we build systems that do repeated finance and operations work for businesses, such as posting sales into Tally and making reports. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean YSS Ventures LLP.

Registered office: 2, Meritt House, New Shakti Nagar Road, Off Behram Baug, Near Police Chowkey, Near Parsi Colony, Jogeshwari (West), Mumbai 400102, Maharashtra, India.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), we are the data fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy.

What we collect

  • When you contact us. If you message us on WhatsApp, call us or email us, we receive your name, phone number or email address as your app shows it to us, and whatever you write, say or attach.
  • If you become a client. The contact and billing details we need to agree the work, deliver it and invoice for it.
  • Technical logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the service. We do not receive or use these logs.
  • What we do not collect. This website has no forms, no sign-up or login, no analytics or advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to you, to discuss and scope the work you asked about and, if you become a client, to deliver that work and invoice for it.

We do not use your details for advertising, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Consent, and taking it back

When you contact us, you consent to our using the details you share for the purposes above. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing hello@yssventures.com. Withdrawing it is as easy as giving it.

Once you withdraw consent, we stop using your data and delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it (for example, records of invoices we have issued). Withdrawal does not affect what we did with your data before it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

We share it only with the service providers that carry it for us, and only for the purposes above: our email provider, WhatsApp, and our phone carrier. Their own privacy policies also apply to messages sent through them.

We may disclose personal data if the law requires it, for example on a lawful request from a government authority.

Client data we handle for our work

When a client asks us to automate work on their own records, such as their accounting data, we handle that data on the client's instructions and under our written agreement with them. The agreement sets out how we protect it. This policy covers the data we collect for ourselves.

Our LinkedIn page

We run a LinkedIn page for YSS Ventures LLP. When we connect that page to tools through LinkedIn's API, we use the access only to publish and manage our own page's posts and to read our own page's analytics.

We do not store LinkedIn members' profile data beyond what is needed to show comments on our own posts, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that. We do not use LinkedIn data for advertising, and we never sell or share it.

LinkedIn's own privacy policy applies to your use of LinkedIn.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we are talking with you about the work. If nothing comes of it, we delete them once they are no longer needed for that purpose, or sooner if you ask.

For clients, we keep the records we need to deliver the work, and the business and tax records the law requires us to keep, for as long as the law requires.

How we protect it

Only the people in our team who need personal data to do the work can see it. Our accounts are password-protected, and this website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the DPDP Act requires.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access. Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correction and erasure. Ask us to correct, complete, update or delete your personal data.
  • Grievance. Raise a complaint with us about how we handle your data, and get an answer.
  • Nomination. Name another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, email hello@yssventures.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act, so that we never hand your data to someone else.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us their details, email hello@yssventures.com and we will delete them.

Grievances and contact

For any question or complaint about this policy or your personal data, email hello@yssventures.com, or write to us at our registered office above. We will reply as soon as we can, and within the time the law allows.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
