Questions

What kind of work do you automate?

Finance and accounting work that repeats: getting sales into Tally, reconciliations, daily and monthly reports, labour bills, credit notes and attendance.

Do we need to replace our software?

Usually not. We work with the tools you already use, like Tally and Google Sheets.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the workflow. You get a timeline with the proposal, before any work starts.

Can you work with our team?

Yes. We work alongside whoever runs the process today.

What do you need from us to start?

A walkthrough of how the work is done today, and a few real examples of the data involved.

What happens on the first call?

You show us how the work is done today, and we tell you what can run on its own. The first conversation is free, and nothing starts until you say yes.
